About Us — TERRABYTE Platform

Department of Land Resources • Ministry of Rural Development • Government of India
RFCTLARR Act 2013

TERRABYTE (National Land Acquisition & Management System) is the unified digital public infrastructure engineered to bring complete transparency, algorithmic accountability, and direct citizen benefit transfer to land acquisition proceedings across India.

Operating in strict compliance with the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement (RFCTLARR) Act, 2013, the platform bridges Central Infrastructure Ministries (NHAI, MoRTH, DFCCIL, Indian Railways, MoPNG), State Revenue Departments, and over 1.2 Crore land-owning citizens (Khatedars).

⚖️ Statutory Workflow Automation
Automated milestones from Section 4 SIA to Section 38 physical land handover.
🗺️ Geo-Referenced Cadastre
Satellite imagery overlay with sub-meter cadastral polygon verification.
✍️ Aadhaar e-Sign & DSC
Legally enforceable ₹100 E-Stamp deeds signed via Aadhaar OTP and Class-3 DSC.
💳 Zero-Leakage PFMS DBT
Direct disbursement of statutory compensation (Base + 100% Solatium + 12% Interest).

नागरिक भूमि विवरण व प्रतिकर पूछताछ / Citizen Land Inquiry

Verify cadastral RoR records, track statutory Section 15 objections, or calculate compensation entitlement under RFCTLARR Act 2013.

⚖️ Statutory Compensation Formula
Total Award = (B × M) + S + A
• B = Base Market Value (Sec 26)
• M = Multiplier (1.0x Urban to 2.0x Rural)
• S = 100% Solatium (Sec 30)
• A = 12% Additional Market Value

All compensation awards are directly credited to beneficiary bank accounts through PFMS DBT with zero intermediary commission.

📋 Required Supporting Documents
  • Updated Record of Rights (7/12 / Khasra / Jamabandi)
  • Aadhaar Card (Identity & Biometric Verification)
  • Cancelled Cheque / Bank Passbook Copy (DBT Mandate)
  • Mutation Extract / Registered Sale Deed
